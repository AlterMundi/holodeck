## Multiscale materials and lighting

[Holodeck #20](https://github.com/AlterMundi/holodeck/issues/20) tracks the receiving work. Modern engines layer detail maps and world-aligned sampling, and recommend baking expensive procedural graphs when the result permits it. Randomized tiling is applicable to isotropic signals whose contrast needs to survive a blend. View-dependent stochastic filtering has a different purpose and requires temporal stability the lightweight client cannot assume.

- [Epic detail and world-aligned texturing](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/texturing-material-functions-in-unreal-engine)
- [Epic utility materials and baking guidance](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/utility-material-expressions-in-unreal-engine)
- [Deliot, Heitz and Neyret histogram-preserving texture synthesis](https://unity-grenoble.github.io/website/demo/2020/10/16/demo-histogram-preserving-blend-synthesis.html)
- [NVIDIA filtering after shading](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/rtr/publication/pharr2024stochtex/stochtex.pdf)

A focused Claude Code Fable 5.2 High review completed before this implementation. It located the repeated signal in the periodic one-metre detail layer, warned about mip bleed across material-role tiles and about destroying directional grain, and recommended testing decorrelated fine and room-scale domains without additional shipped maps before heavier stochastic synthesis. The first candidate keeps the baked room irradiance, baked structural seams and specular response. It unpacks the existing five grayscale tile cores into an isolated mipmapped texture-array layer per role. Gradient-aware samples add independent fine and coarse variation inside the prior modulation amplitude, with the steel grain direction retained. The existing floor reflection and other appearance variants use their existing paths. Start validation with matched Orbital layered floor, deck and wall/shelf views in normal and lightweight, and measure identical camera motion on each client. Evaluate pattern recurrence and visible silhouette, highlight and contact lighting before proposing the selected look. Claude Code Opus 5.5 High reviewed the actual material diff. It identified cross-grain in the coarse steel band, mid-distance contrast loss and implicit anisotropy changes. The correction keeps both steel bands axis-aligned, restores primary detail weight and preserves the source anisotropy. Runtime canvas readback and projected normal boundaries remain explicit portability checks. The corrected package `1fd0283a0c64` at `d3c42bf` was 33,454,080 bytes, 100,352 bytes under the cap. The exact package rendered Orbital Layered in both modes without a shader error in controlled Chromium. Three paired camera positions and a moving sequence ran in both modes. Software-rendered p95 rose from 349.9 to 383.3 ms in normal mode and from 216.6 to 233.3 ms in lightweight. These are fixture measurements, not hardware frame times. The baseline and candidate include different HUD implementations, so the results do not isolate the shader cost. The benchmark and the visual quality bar remain open before promoting a material. Current live digest readback is still `f46d93f436be`.
