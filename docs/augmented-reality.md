# Candidate creation: shared augmented territory

Human proposal, 2026-11-17: map the tribe's physical environment in 3D and add a
shared augmentation layer. Humans can encounter that layer in place; daimons can
access the same situation through appropriate native perception and interfaces.
People without an augmentation viewer see the ordinary physical environment.
This is a proposed Holodeck creation, not a new capture/deployment already running.

The first candidate game is capture-the-flag or domination with QR markers on
walls. Domination is the simpler initial exemplar: a few named stations, teams,
explicit captures and server-owned control/score time. A QR identifies a station;
it does not require a complete reconstructed mesh, headset or precise AR tracking.

## Shared state and experience

- Each physical marker names a stable station ID. Marker identity, physical anchor,
  virtual representation and current game entity are related explicitly.
- A human scan produces an attributed observation/action request. The game
  authority confirms or rejects the capture, orders an event and updates control.
  Clients and native perception consume that same event/state.
- A phone can initially show a station card and map; persistent overlaid geometry
  needs a qualified tracking/anchor implementation. QR recognition alone is not
  evidence of stable six-degree-of-freedom alignment or physical presence.
- Daimons participate through direct native perception, game operations and their
  chosen projection. They do not emulate a phone camera or human scanning UI.
  Define the shared team activity and physical/virtual roles as game rules; do not
  assume an unimplemented physical ability or reduce daimons to narrators.
- Room/run, station/entity revision, control events and scoring clock are shared.
  Different displays or text maps must not invent different owners or scores.
  Simulation time progresses independently of native model turns.

A casual first trial needs no new identity/account service: reuse existing tribal
admission, actor rights and explicit participation. QR payloads contain public
station references, never credentials or authority. A scan is sufficient for
the chosen casual rule only if that is the declared game; it is not cryptographic
proof that someone physically visited the wall.

## Progression

1. Confirm one named physical marker against its shared virtual station.
2. Play one bounded short domination round with two teams, live control changes
   and a real native daimon turn grounded in those events.
3. Add consistent spatial augmentation and a small physical map.
4. Extend to the richer 3D map, selected sensors and recorded observations.

Keep live sensor observations, old recordings, inferred reconstructions, simulated
objects and invented augmentations distinguishable, with source/time/uncertainty.
No sensors, camera recording, physical mapping, background attention or game server
is activated by this proposal. Initial software work remains the Holodeck situated
world foundation and its native conversation integration.
